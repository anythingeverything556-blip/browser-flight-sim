# F27 preview revision 2

- **Aircraft**: one white F27 Mk500. The exterior is 1,100,828 bytes; the higher-detail English cockpit is 4,488,904 bytes. The exterior loads first. The cockpit downloads in the background after the exterior and airport data are ready (or on first entry for Data Saver), with decoded textures uploaded before view switch. Enter using V or F27 Systems → Go to cockpit.
- **Seating**: the seated source camera is 8 cm lower, at a centered heading, with −15 to +15 cm of additional seat adjustment in F27 Systems.
- **Propellers and stance**: the original blades and spinner remain visible per engine, with motion blur and a visually readable shutter-strobe approximation at higher RPM rather than a missing disc or static cross. Turbine/propeller mechanics retain physical RPM values. Gear alignment uses decoded tire vertices and the real spring compression, not assumed model datum. Contact shading is visible on low quality as well.
- **Runway**: use the terrain beneath the tires. A local terrain inset follows the same airport elevation used for wheel contact, even when coarse scenery has the wrong height. Its runway markings come from public airport/runway data, not satellite photography. It fades when detailed satellite imagery and the underlying rendered terrain both line up.
- **Controls and lighting**: a dedicated in-flight F27 Systems panel has navigation/beacon/wing/landing/taxi switches, white and red dome lights, instrument dimming, seat height, engine start/stop, and doors. Animated landing/taxi beams illuminate terrain in addition to aircraft surfaces; most visible at dusk or night.
- **Audio**: the F27 engine and cockpit mix uses the supplied original Wwise recordings converted to AAC, not a synthesized turboprop engine. Includes independent left/right inside/outside RPM layers and selected starter, wind, roll, inverter and switch effects. The web mix and sample looping are adaptations of the source; this is not the entire original Wwise/MSFS runtime. Browser sound still requires an interaction and can be muted from Sound.

Tests: `npm test` runs the asset wiring, actual GLB ground/propeller geometry and original sample/playback regression scripts. `SMOKE_AIRCRAFT=f27 npm run test:smoke` runs the local production build and checks staged cockpit, audio decoding, systems switches, ground-light uniforms and the fallback runway. Scenery provider availability and real-device performance still vary. No main-branch or production changes were requested.
