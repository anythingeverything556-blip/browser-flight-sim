# F27 gear / cockpit response follow-up — 23 November 2026

## Causes and corrections

The supplied exterior v3 and cockpit v2 GLBs contain **gear deployment in source frames 0–100**, followed by suspension data extending to frame 200. Previously every animation was normalized to its own duration. Most hinges therefore reached down halfway through the commanded travel while shorter linkage clips ran on a different clock; the nose suspension-only clips then crossed a discontinuity at frame 101. All deployment/linkage clips now sample a common 100/24-second authoring interval. Nose Dump1, Dump2 and SupportV3 keep their authored rest angles; the existing physical spring/contact travel remains active and independently calibrated.

`GEAR_Steering` is a 360° lookup (0° at its first key, 180° at its midpoint). The exported bind pose is also 180°, so simply retaining the bind pose is not a neutral fix. The visual now uses the first source key as neutral and applies the FDM's speed-dependent steering angle, not a normalized whole-turn animation. Both representations center progressively with retraction.

The FDM keeps the existing 7-second down / 17-second up travel. Mid-cycle reversal changes only the command, not position. Repeated identical commands do not drain air. Ground retraction and low-pressure refusal remain enforced. A tiny numerical endpoint tolerance prevents an extra frame at the end and emits exactly one gear-lock event. Starts reset to a completed appropriate pose. The cockpit gear selector follows the command immediately; gear lamps still follow the actual position.

F27 Systems has a gear button and live state/percentage/remaining time. The HUD stays in transit until the actual end stop, then says GEAR DOWN or GEAR UP. The command toast distinguishes insufficient pneumatic pressure from weight on wheels.

## Cockpit optimization

848 static, unskinned, non-animated **leaf meshes** retain their authored local matrices. Animated nodes are excluded by every source track's target name/UUID. Visibility, materials, annunciators and world matrices stay live. No model, texture, lighting, resolution, shadow-selection or gauge-rate downgrade. The visible representation's matrices are propagated once after pose sampling with current camera-relative ancestors; the renderer propagates final suspension/prop transforms as usual.

The decoded-asset update-plus-render-matrix regression records 3,503 local matrix compositions with these leaves unfrozen versus 1,807 optimized: **1,696 fewer compositions**, with identical final static world transforms. This measures CPU work only, not end-to-end FPS or hardware performance. Exterior local-matrix policy is unchanged.

Press G on the ground: it must stay down and explain the ground lock. Steer each way at low taxi speed; the fork turns the same way as the aircraft and returns to center.
2. Use an airborne cruise start. Open F27 Systems → Aircraft. Press G / Extend gear: the selector moves down immediately; travel reports extending, percentage-down and seconds remaining. At 3.5 s it should still be about halfway, not already fully deployed. At 7 s it remains fully down with no second movement/snap. Wait a few more seconds to confirm it stays down.
