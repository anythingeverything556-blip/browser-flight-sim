## Unreleased (hands-off assist off: the Skyhawk and Vision Jet answer the stick again)
Owner report: with Hands-off assist switched off the aircraft cannot be flown.
- **Cause:** with the option off, every frame reset the assist's state *and* the pilot's slewed stick (`stick172`) after the stick had been read. The next frame slewed again from zero, so the Skyhawk and the Vision Jet got one frame's worth of slew: about 4 % elevator and 7 % aileron at 60 fps, whatever the pilot held. The Realistic flying mode switches the option off, so it had the same problem. The SR-71 was not affected (its stick is not slewed).
- **Fix:** the per-frame GA controls moved to `gaControlFrame` (handsoff.js). Switching the assist off, a crash, slew or pause now resets only the assist; the stick resets at spawn.
- Test: `handsoff-option-test` (full roll and full back stick, assist off and on, in both aircraft; it fails on the old code with 0.07 aileron and 0.04 elevator).

## Unreleased (clouds anchored to the Earth, overcast and rain weather, rain on the GA windshields)
Owner report: the clouds follow the same pattern and appear in the same places; they do not look like real clouds; rain does not look real; no rain on the Vision Jet and Cessna windshields.
- **Clouds anchored to the Earth.** Cause: the field was drawn at (position from the world origin) - wind x page time, and the origin moves to every spawn, so every flight, at every airport, on every day, had the identical cloud layout round the aircraft. `cloudAnchor()` (cloudlayer.js) adds the origin's absolute map position (wrapped for 32-bit GPU precision) and a per-day shift, plus a per-day phase of the clouds' growth: the same place on the same day shows the same sky, other places and days do not. Volumetric clouds, their ground shadows, the in-cloud check and the Low-preset billboard clouds all use it. The shape lookups are also shifted by the large-scale coverage field, so neighbouring repeats of the 64^3 noise tile no longer line up into a pattern.
- **Overcast and rain look overcast.** Full cover now closes into a continuous deck (the cumulus contrast change of the last release, and the coverage field's clear areas, put blue sky over Rain and Overcast). Under a closed deck the sun drops to 18 % and the sky light turns grey and dimmer (sky.js `overcast`, set from the cover above the camera), and the distance haze greys: rain was lit like a clear day with hard shadows.
- **Overcast cloud light.** Seen from below, a thick deck is lit by sunlight scattered many times, so it is grey: the clear-sky ambient alone made the Rain deck a blue ceiling. The cloud ambient now desaturates with depth and gets a neutral multiple-scattering floor. The aircraft's own ground shadow fades under overcast.
- **Denser rain.** 30,000 streaks in a 60 m box (was 12,000 in 80 m, about 6x the density), fading out over 45 m.
- **Rain on the glass of the Vision Jet and the Skyhawk.** `GlassWater` (beads, runners, refraction) was only attached to the SR-71 canopy. The pattern's canopy height is now a parameter (`ridge`): the Vision Jet's windshield and side windows (not the frit) and the Skyhawk's windshield, side and rear windows get the same drops.
- Tests: `cloud-anchor-test`; the C172 and SF50 visual tests check that the glass wets in rain, seen from the seat, and dries after.

Owner report: the planes on the map took very long to load; the SR-71 cockpit had too many overlapping parts and did not look like the real one; fix the clouds.
