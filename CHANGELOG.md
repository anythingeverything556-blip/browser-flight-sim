# Changelog

## Unreleased
- **Cockpit frame rate: hidden-surface mask.** Measured with per-draw GPU timer queries in the software renderer (relative cost only; a real GPU is still to be
  checked): the exterior skin (`SR71_Fuselage_mesh001` and `SR71_ChineZone`, physical paint + clearcoat + procedural wear, double-sided) was 58 % of the cockpit
  frame's draw time although it is visible in only 1-15 % of the pixels. The logarithmic depth buffer makes every shader write its own depth, so the GPU cannot
  reject a hidden fragment early: the skin shader ran for the inside of the fuselage, the ground and the sky behind the panel, glare shield and consoles.
  Each cockpit frame now draws the opaque cockpit parts into a full-size one-channel depth mask (`src/occlusion.js`, 153 parts; the pass is about 0.5 % of the frame in the software renderer), shrinks it by
  a pixel, and the skin, the aircraft's other lit materials, the cockpit's lit materials, the terrain and the trees return early for a fragment behind it. The
  early-out still writes the fragment's depth, so the depth buffer (clouds, AO, glass read it) is exactly what it was. Cockpit view only: elsewhere the test is a
  uniform-gated branch. Software-renderer result at 960x540, Low, on final approach (6 frames each): median draw time per frame 5.0 s -> 3.0 s (-40 %), best
  frame -25 %; pictures at six look angles differ in at most 47 of 518,400 pixels by at most 19/255 (hairline cracks between cockpit meshes), and in the High
  preset's post pipeline in at most 47 pixels by at most 10/255; two unmasked renders are identical. The real-GPU gain is not measured.
  An earlier half-size mask let one-pixel gaps between cockpit parts through (about 100 pixels), so it is full size. F3 shows the mask size and a `cockpit mask`
  GPU pass; F10 has a `cockpit hidden-surface mask off` state; `tools/cockpitcost.mjs` repeats the measurement; `test/occlusion-test.mjs` checks the shader
  patch against every three.js template it is applied to.
- **Low preset: the droplet overlay is not drawn while the canopy is dry.** It was a transparent shader drawn over the whole glass every frame, rain or not.
- **Fixed: the Mach dial needle was drawn wrong.** A repeated vertex in the needle path cancelled the long half, so only the short tail showed (pointing down at Mach 1.55, up-left at 3.28). Now a simple polygon on a 0-3.5 dial; `test/mach-dial-test.mjs` guards it.
- **Imagery loading is honest and sharper.** Start waits until the ring of ground around the jet shows its imagery and the tile under the jet is at zoom 15 or finer (or the loader has nothing more to fetch, i.e. as sharp as the provider gets), up to 45 s; the loading bar shows that real progress. Scenery for a runway starts loading the moment it is picked in the menu, not when Start is pressed. The terrain level-of-detail clamp I added earlier (it made the picture softer at 200 %) is removed. Esri fetch slots 10 -> 12 (it is HTTP/1.1, so about 12 is its ceiling).
- **New high-resolution regional imagery** (free government tile servers, CORS-open, checked from the sandbox): swisstopo SWISSIMAGE (10 cm), PDOK Netherlands (8 cm), basemap.at Austria (30 cm), GSI Japan. Esri is still used everywhere else; in the places you named (Geneva, Moscow, Delhi, Mumbai, Rio, Lima, Nairobi, Johannesburg, Tokyo, Sydney, Cairo) Esri has real tiles up to zoom 17-19, and rural India, Russia and the Amazon are as sharp as Esri itself is there.
- **Water physics:** the terrain water mask (the one that draws the water) now has a coarse grid per tile and `terrain.waterAt(lat, lon)`. Wheels or belly in the water (not on a runway) end the flight: above 90 kt the jet breaks up on the surface, below it floods and sinks; the wreck sinks below the surface.
- **Landing aid** (Options, on by default; the pitch keys switch it off): gear down and below 150 ft with a sink rate, the elevator follows a flare profile with a fast vertical loop. Offline on the real flight model a hands-off 3 degree approach touched down at 4.1 m/s; with the aid 1.3-2.5 m/s at 165-200 kt and 3-4.5 degree glides (a hard landing counts as a crash above 5.5 m/s). `test/landing-test.mjs`.
- **Flight assist is off by default** and explained in the first-flight guide and the help sheet. Terrain relief shading is fixed at 100 %.
- **Plume / vapour at the horizon:** ambient occlusion no longer darkens additive effects, and the jet-vapour sprites are marked in the scene alpha like the plume, so neither is dimmed differently over ground and sky.
- **Clouds:** cover and base ease toward the chosen weather over ~25 s (they used to re-roll the whole sky when a preset or METAR loaded); a large-scale (110 km) weather octave and a third noise scale at another angle break up the repeating pattern; the same change is in the renderer, the ground cloud shadows and the in-cloud detector.
- **Fixed: rain droplets never reached the canopy glass inside cloud.** The in-cloud amount was applied after the aircraft had been updated for the frame and reset at the top of the next frame. It now feeds the glass before the update. Falling streaks are no longer drawn in the cockpit view while in cloud (only the droplets on the glass), and are faint outside.
- **Afterburner plume and the cloud layer:** the cloud composite no longer paints over additive effects (the target's alpha rises above 1 where they are drawn), so the plume keeps its brightness where it overlaps the horizon cloud band.
- **Clouds evolve:** the cloud noise drifts slowly through its own height (period 300 s), in the cloud shader, the ground cloud-shadow map and the in-cloud detector together, and wind aloft is never below 9 m/s.
- F10 (with F3): cycles a test view that hides the terrain, the trees and buildings, or the aircraft and cockpit, so the GPU time in F3 shows which one costs the frames on a given machine.
- **Trees appear sooner:** 5 satellite-tile fetches per tree chunk (was 2, and 1 whenever more than 30 terrain tiles were queued; now 3), and up to 3 decode workers on machines with 4+ cores (was 2).
- Adaptive resolution is off again for everyone once (an F3 report showed it still on and at its 55 % floor), its floor is now 75 %, and the option is unchecked by default.
- Terrain level of detail follows the screen's own pixel height (capped at 1.25x), not the supersampled buffer, so 200 % no longer asks for roughly twice the tiles, textures and triangles.
- Canopy glass is a flat transparent material instead of a lit PBR one (it covers the whole forward view two layers deep).
- The hooded Mach indicator is live: a 0-3.5 dial with a needle that follows the flight model.
- Flight assist (options, on by default): when the stick is released the jet levels its wings and eases vertical speed to zero over about 10 s, with stall and overspeed protection; stick input takes over immediately. Offline rig: recovers from 4-60 degree pitch / 25-60 degree bank releases at M0.9-M3 and 5,000-78,000 ft with no stall and no crash.
- Clouds: the density detail fades out as the march step grows (the shimmer of distant clouds); the aircraft cuts a tunnel through cloud along its recent path (wider and longer-lived with afterburner); rain streaks and glass droplets appear inside cloud (stronger in a thunderhead).
- Stars are soft round dots scaled to the pixel ratio instead of 1-2 pixel squares (flicker as the camera turns).
- Imagery colour balance falls back to the nearest ancestor that has colours when the parent tile is gone, so a tile is not left unbalanced beside balanced neighbours.
- **Fixed: grainy dome of fake cloud over the whole upper sky when flying above the cloud layer** (visible in the owner's Mach 3.28 / 73,000 ft screenshot). Looking up from above the layer, both ray intersections were behind the camera, the march start stayed negative and the shader marched backwards through the clouds behind the aircraft. Before/after: `docs/img-sky-noise-before.png`, `docs/img-sky-noise-after.png`.
- Autopilot: speed-scheduled climb (about 370 KEAS up to Mach 3.2; reaches 78,700 ft at Mach 3.2 in about 21 minutes in the offline rig instead of hanging on the minimum speed), tests for gusty air, 180-degree turns at 150 kt and Mach 3, and the climb.
- Cockpit panel: static artwork at 4096x2304 (2048x1152 fallback), needles/digits on a separate 1536x864 overlay in the same material, redrawn at most 20 Hz and only when a displayed value changes. New `src/systems.js` (hydraulics, electrics, oxygen, cabin pressure, oil, fuel groups) replaces fixed gauge values; `docs/gauge-audit.md` lists every needle and its source.
- METAR parsing (CAVOK, metres/SM, VV, M temperatures, QNH/altimeter, weather codes to rain intensity), ATC phraseology and ATIS, traffic back-off and an optional relay (`docs/traffic-setup.md`), regional tree variants (`src/treeclimate.js`).
- Aircraft sun-shadow map is redrawn only when the aircraft, sun, gear or elevons change or the aircraft moves relative to the camera.
- Render resolution now defaults to **200 %** (slider 75-250 %, effective ratio capped at 3). Costs that scale with the screen's own pixels rather than the supersampled buffer: hardware MSAA is dropped when supersampling already gives 4+ samples per pixel (ratio/native >= 1.75; 2x at >= 1.25), and the volumetric-cloud march target is never larger than the screen's own resolution. Existing saved settings are moved to 200 % once.
- Shadow cascades are redrawn by rule instead of by frame count: only when the camera nears the cascade's edge, the view turns or zooms, the sun moves by a fraction of a texel, the caster set changes (rate limited), or the aircraft may be in it (cascade 0 every frame, cascade 1 every 2nd near the ground). The caster material swap is skipped on frames where nothing is redrawn. F3 shows done/skipped per cascade.
- Cloud shadows on the ground cross-fade between successive maps (0.33 s) and use a smoothed ground height, so the map re-render (about 3 Hz) no longer steps the ground shading.
- F3 now splits GPU time by pass (shadow maps, scene, AO, glass, clouds, composite, bloom, SMAA) where the browser exposes timer queries. F5-F9 switch world shadows / AO / clouds / SMAA / bloom off for testing on real hardware.
- Loading: physics starts after 2.5 s (was 5 s) of scenery waiting and at 60 % scenery (was 85 %); the sharp ground under the jet is still required. `public/_headers` gives hashed assets a one-year immutable cache and the model, decoder and airport data sensible lifetimes; the GLB and airports.json are preloaded from index.html.
- Autopilot: angle-of-attack (stall) protection, minimum-speed floor derived from the flight model's lift curve and the current weight, overspeed / Mach / inlet-temperature protection, anti-windup, status text (`STALL PROTECT`, `OVERSPEED PROTECT`, `CIT LIMIT`, `SPEED LOW`, `CANNOT CLIMB — LOW SPEED`), and a recovery mode on **U**.
- Redundant per-frame work removed: the 2D rain canvas is left alone unless it has something to show, the HUD is skipped when hidden and redrawn at 30 Hz, the cockpit panel is skipped while no gauge changes.
- F3: render-target sizes, screen / pixel-ratio line, redundant-work counters; Shift+F3 diagnostics to the clipboard; F4 1:1 pixel test.
- Tests: closed-loop autopilot scenarios, dirty-flag logic. `tools/netaudit.mjs` network audit.
