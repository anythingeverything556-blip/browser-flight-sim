    Typical sortie

    1. Press 9: the J58s take ~6 s to spool and the afterburners light with a green TEB flash.
    2. Rotate gently at ~210 kt, pitch 10°, gear up (G) once climbing.
    3. In Autopilot set Mach 3.2 and 80,000 ft. Climb and acceleration take about 15 minutes.
    4. Keep sideslip small above Mach 2.3 or an inlet can unstart — the jet yaws hard until it restarts.
    5. Plan the arrival on the Map, pick the arrival runway, then use NAV and APPR.
    6. Fly final at ~175 kt, touch down below 160 kt, chute (C) and brakes.

    Cockpit

    The front panel is live: ADI with flight director and glideslope, HSI with bearing/DME to the active waypoint and localizer, KEAS/Mach, triple display, altimeter, VSI, g, AoA, clock, fuel, gear lights, both engines' RPM, EGT, nozzle and fuel flow, spike and bypass positions, CIT and annunciators.
