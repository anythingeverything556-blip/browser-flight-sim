# Earthwing: clear starts, flap controls, and fleet autopilots

## What pilots will notice in the game

- **Weather:** new/ordinary starts use Clear and do not fetch live weather automatically. Old saves from when live weather was on by default are migrated without carrying their fog into the next start. You can still pick **Fog** or other poor-weather presets on purpose, or opt in to live METAR in **Weather**. A deliberate preset is not replaced by the automatic request. **Clear** removes the screen fog veil immediately; normal distant terrain haze remains.
- **F27 flaps:** from **F27 Systems → Aircraft**, select **UP (0°), 11.5°, 16.5° (T/O), 26.5°, or 40° (FULL)**. With the flight view focused, press and release **F** to advance one step, cycling from FULL to UP, or **Shift+F** to retract one step. The panel shows the command and actual angle; a full sweep is 15 simulation seconds. The cockpit lever clicks to the commanded detent immediately; the wing and indicator follow actual travel. Normal runway starts intentionally use 16.5°. An explicit airborne/teleport setup no longer silently substitutes a speed-derived flap setting. The existing 145 KIAS model flap warning is unchanged.
- **Fleet flaps:** C172, DA40, SF50, F27 and Il-76 cycle from FULL to UP on a fresh **F** press; **Shift+F** retracts one notch and stops at UP. Holding or repeating a key does not advance another notch. The archival A380's **F** stops at FULL; the SR-71 has no flaps. Do not add flaps or slats to variants without them.
- **Autopilot:** use **K** or the **Autopilot** panel in flight. The F27 Mk500, DA40 NG and C172SP now offer representative two-axis fits with HDG, NAV, ALT, V/S, and tuned ILS APPR; C172 is labeled as optional equipment. These are simulator panel controls, not new physical avionics on the cockpit models. **You set power (E/Q)**. No automatic throttle, FLCH, takeoff, go-around, recovery, or autoland on those three. Manual stick/trim disconnects, and the A/P disconnects at the approach minimum. The existing SR-71, SF50 and Il-76 implementations remain, including the Vision Jet-specific Safe Return.
- **Systems:** Shift+I now operates the C172 ignition (OFF/BOTH) and Vision Jet engine master/fuel cutoff using the existing engine models. DA40 and F27 controls are retained; starters and full checklists are not modeled. The F27 panel can switch both modeled radio altimeters; the display covers 0–2,500 ft AGL, switches off above 5,000 ft AGL, and requires a new ON action. Optional spoken landing heights are a separate game aid.

## F27 climb diagnosis

This is a simulator measurement, not a flight-manual performance chart. At ~18.35 t, starting at 1,500 m, and holding approximately 138 KIAS with modeled 14,200 RPM climb power, the actual FDM gave about **+967 ft/min clean**, **+541 ft/min with 16.5° takeoff flaps and gear up**, and **−1,069 ft/min with full flaps and gear down**. These are three separate controlled trials, not a promise about a particular takeoff. In a separate clean run starting at 6,000 m at the modeled maximum takeoff weight, climb fell to about **+230 ft/min**. Pulling the nose up without holding airspeed cannot replace power or remove gear/flap drag.

The F27 trim solver now projects body-axis propeller thrust along and across the flight path and uses the true speed corresponding to indicated takeoff speed at the field. The model's rated power, aerodynamic drag tables and operating limits were not raised. Separate manual clean-climb tests passed on the C172, DA40, SF50, and Il-76 using aircraft-appropriate speeds, mass, and power; the existing SR-71 climb/profile regressions still apply. No certified six-aircraft handling or exact real-flight replay is claimed.
